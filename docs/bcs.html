Payment Engine

AI Intelligence — Powered by Google Gemini

The AI engine analyzes your real sales data — velocity, batch expiry, margins — and makes specific recommendations. Not generic tips. Actual product names, actual quantities, actual profit calculations.
🏷️ AI Offer Suggestions
  • Identifies products expiring before they sell
  • Flags slow-moving & dead stock
  • Suggests: % discount, bundle, buy X get Y
  • Never suggests an offer below margin
  • Shows urgency (High/Medium/Low)
  • Predicts expected impact in units
📋 AI Reorder Suggestions
  • Calculates days of stock remaining per product
  • Priority: Urgent (under 7 days)
  • Priority: Normal (7–20 days)
  • Priority: Low (20–30 days)
  • Exact suggested quantities
  • Total estimated cost per order
  • Save as Purchase Order, track to delivery

Online Store & Delivery Module

Activate in one click. BCS creates a public-facing storefront with your store's URL automatically. Customers browse and order. The delivery flow is fully automated — from order to driver dispatch.
🌐
Auto Storefront
Your store URL is live the moment you activate. Product catalog with images, search, categories. Mobile-first responsive design. Share via WhatsApp or print QR code.
⚡
Cart Auto-Injection
When admin accepts an online order, the customer's cart automatically appears in the cashier's POS screen — no retyping, no errors.
📱
Telegram Driver Alert
When order dispatches, driver gets a Telegram message: customer name, full address, item list, total, and payment method.
🔄
Live Order Tracking
Customer sees real-time status: Pending → Accepted → Out for Delivery → Delivered. No phone calls needed from either side.

Café Management

Live Floor Map
Visual table grid. Real-time status: Free (green), Occupied (red), Customer QR order (orange). Drag tables to match your physical layout.

Full-screen kitchen dashboard. Audio beep on new orders. Items cycle: Pending → Preparing → Ready → Served. "📱 Customer" badge on QR orders.

Split any table's bill across any number of guests, each paying a different method. Service charge, discount, and split — all in one checkout flow.
