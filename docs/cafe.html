Floor Map & Kitchen Display
Features 01 & 02

Live Floor Map
& Kitchen Display System

Floor Map

A visual grid of every table in the café. All staff see the same map in real time — table status changes the moment an order is placed or a bill is paid.
🟢
Free Table
Green — no open order. Click to start a new order and seat guests. Table turns occupied immediately.
🔴
Occupied Table
Red — has an active order. Click to view/add items, check item statuses, or generate the bill.
🟠
Customer QR Order
Orange — a customer placed an order from their phone. Items appear in kitchen automatically.
⚙️
Layout Editor
Drag and position tables on the map to match your physical floor plan. Changes save instantly.

Table Order Features

  • Add items with quantity & notes
  • Per-item modifier notes ("no sugar")
  • Send items to kitchen
  • Add more items at any time
  • Transfer items to another table
  • Merge two tables into one order
  • View item kitchen status
  • Mark items as served

Kitchen Display System (KDS)

A full-screen dashboard for the kitchen. No paper tickets. No shouting across the counter. Every order update is live — and the system alerts kitchen staff with an audio beep the instant a new order arrives.
📋
Order Cards
Each table's order appears as a card. Items show their status: Pending → Preparing → Ready → Served. Click any item to cycle its status.
🔔
Audio Alert
When a new order arrives in the kitchen (from staff or customer QR), the KDS plays an audio beep via the Web Audio API.
📱
Customer Badge
Items ordered by customers via QR show a "📱 Customer" badge — so kitchen staff know the source without asking anyone.
⚡
Real-Time Updates
When a server marks an item "Served," the customer's order tracker updates live. When all items are served, the table is ready for billing.

Bill Management & Reservations
Features 03 & 04

Smart Billing
& Reservations

Bill & Checkout

🧮
Service Charge
Apply a configurable service charge percentage to the bill. Shown separately on the receipt so the customer sees exactly what they're paying.
🏷️
Bill Discount
Apply a flat or percentage discount at bill time. Useful for loyalty members, VIP customers, or table promotions.
👥
Split Bill
Divide the total across any number of guests. Each guest sees their share. Each person can pay with a different method (cash, card).
💳
Multiple Payment Methods
Cash, card, bank transfer — all supported. The checkout creates a Sale record, closes the order, and frees the table automatically.

Reservations

A full reservation management system. See all bookings by date, manage no-shows, and convert any reservation into an active table order with one click.
  • Create reservations: guest name, phone, date, time, table, guest count, notes
  • Calendar view — browse reservations by any date
  • Status tracking: Confirmed → Seated → Cancelled → No-Show
  • One-click "Seat" — converts reservation directly into an active order on the floor map
  • View upcoming reservations for the day on the floor dashboard

Customer QR App & Loyalty System
Features 05 & 06

Customer QR App
& Loyalty Points System

Customers scan a QR code on the table and order directly from their phone. No app download. No waiting for a server. And for loyal customers — every order earns points redeemable for free items.

Two Entry Modes

🚀 Quick Menu (Guest)

No account needed. Scan the table QR, browse the menu, place an order. The order goes directly to the kitchen and appears on the floor map. After ordering, the customer can play games for entertainment (no points).

⭐ Play & Earn (Registered)

Register with phone + password or sign in with Google. Every order earns points. Daily check-ins earn points. Games earn points. Redeem points for free items or discounts on the next order.

How Customers Earn Points

ActionPoints Earned
First visit (welcome bonus)+100 pts
Daily QR check-in+10 pts / day
Order placed (per item × tier multiplier)+10 pts per item
Rate your order (1–5 stars)+5 pts
Play a game (once per day for points)10–500 pts

Rewards Redemption

The café admin creates rewards in the settings panel (free item, % discount, flat discount). Before placing an order, the customer taps "Use Points" to choose a reward. The discount is applied automatically to their order total.

Live Order Tracking

After placing an order, the customer sees a live status screen that updates as the kitchen prepares each item: Received → Preparing → Ready → Served. Once served, they are prompted to rate the order and play a game to earn more points.

5 Loyalty Games
Feature 07

5 Built-In Loyalty
Mini-Games

Every registered customer can play one game per day to earn points. After that, games remain available for entertainment. This keeps customers engaged with your café app long after their coffee is finished.
🎮 Games Rule
Each game can be played once per day for points. After the daily play, the game switches to entertainment mode (unlimited plays, no points). This prevents abuse while keeping customers entertained. A "For Fun" badge appears on subsequent plays so customers always know.
🎰
Slot Machine
Three reels with coffee-themed symbols: ☕ 🧁 🍰 ⭐ 🎲 🍵. Spin to match symbols across the reels. Three matching stars = 500 pts. Three matching = 200 pts. Two matching = 50 pts. No match = 10 pts consolation.
10 – 500 points
🎡
Spin Wheel
8 animated spinning segments with prizes: 25 pts, 50 pts, 75 pts, 100 pts, 200 pts, Free Add-On, Double Order Points, 15 pts. A colorful animated wheel spins and lands on a random prize.
15 – 200 points + special prizes
🎴
Scratch Card
A 3×3 canvas grid. The customer drags their finger to scratch off the surface. Match 3 identical hidden symbols to win: Coin = 50 pts, Gem = 150 pts, Crown = 300 pts.
20 – 300 points
🎁
Mystery Box
Three face-down cards on screen. The customer picks one. It flips over to reveal a surprise: points (50/100/200/300/500) or a "50% off one item" special reward.
30 – 500 points
🧠
Trivia Challenge
3 multiple-choice questions set by the café admin (coffee trivia, menu facts, general knowledge). 30-second timer per question. 30 pts per correct answer + 20 bonus points for getting all 3 right (streak bonus).
30 – 110 points
