💡 Pay Later — Credit Accounts
When a customer cannot pay in full, the balance is saved against their account as a credit debt. The cashier can collect partial or full payments at any later time. Every payment is timestamped and logged. Outstanding balances appear on the dashboard for instant visibility.

Offline Mode

BCS uses a local IndexedDB cache in the browser. If the internet connection drops, the cashier continues selling without interruption. When connectivity returns, all offline transactions sync automatically to the cloud — nothing is lost.

FIFO Automatic Stock Deduction

When a product is sold, BCS automatically deducts stock from the earliest-expiring batch first (First In, First Out). Batches with no expiry date are consumed last. This prevents expired stock from sitting hidden while newer stock is sold.

Shift & Cash Drawer Management

Every cashier opens a shift before selling. Every sale is linked to that shift. At close, the system calculates exactly how much cash should be in the drawer — and flags any discrepancy.
🔓
Opening a Shift
Cashier counts the opening float using denomination entry (×$1, ×$5, ×$10, ×$20). System calculates total automatically. Shift opens with the float recorded.
📈
During the Shift
All sales link to the open shift. Cash In/Out events (e.g., petty cash withdrawal) recorded with reason at any time.
🔒
Closing a Shift
Cashier counts closing cash with denomination breakdown. System calculates Expected Cash and shows the variance (over/short). Any discrepancy is immediately visible.
📊
Shift Report
Full breakdown: Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, Pay Later, Delivery sales. Net revenue after refunds and discounts. Saved permanently for admin review.

Void PIN Protection

The store admin sets a manager PIN. Any sale void requires entering this PIN — preventing cashiers from quietly voiding sales without authorization. Every void is logged in the audit trail with reason and timestamp.

🇱🇧 Lebanese VAT Compliance — 11%

Lebanese tax law requires 11% VAT on most goods, but certain categories are exempt (basic food, medicines, books, agricultural products). BCS handles all of this automatically once configured.

Setup (one time):
  • Set Tax Rate = 11% in Store Settings
  • Enter your VAT Number (TIN)
  • Mark exempt products with the VAT Exempt toggle
On every receipt:
  • Subtotal (before VAT)
  • VAT Amount (11% on non-exempt items)
  • Final Total
  • Your VAT Registration Number
